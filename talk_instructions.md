Text in the game is formatted in the following fashion:
1. Thoughts
    Thoughts are formatted with *italics*.
2. Narration
    Typical text just printed.
3. Talking
    Words spoken by the player or NPC are formatted within quotes with the name of the character at the start.
        Example:
        `"Timmy: Hello world!"`
    
Furthermore, inputs are formatted like so:
1. Yes or no questions
    Formatted as `"QUESTION (Y/N)\n"`.
2. Free-response (ex. what is your name?)
    Formatted as `"QUESTION\n"`
3. Multiple-choice
    Same as free-response `"QUESTION\n"`
